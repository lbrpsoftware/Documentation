# Desktop - AccoWin - Accounting

## 1. Introduction

The accounting module is the heart of **Accowin**. With this module you maintain the full bookkeeping of a dossier: you manage the master files (customers, suppliers and general ledger accounts), book the transactions in the journals and follow up the balances and open items.

The typical workflow has three steps:

1. **Create the master files**: customers (debtors), suppliers (creditors) and general ledger accounts.
2. **Book the transactions**: in the sales, purchase, financial or miscellaneous journal.
3. **Follow up and check**: consult the history and open items, match payments and check the bookkeeping with the reports.

## 2. Parts

- [Master files](MasterFiles/README.md): manage customers (debtors), suppliers (creditors) and general ledger accounts.
- [Journals](Journals/README.md): set up journals and enter transactions in the sales, purchase, financial and miscellaneous journal, including templates and daily receipts.

## 3. Related modules

- **CODA**: read electronic bank statements automatically into the financial journal (see [Coda](../Coda/README.md)).
- **SODA**: read files from the social secretariat into the miscellaneous journal.
- **UBL**: import electronic supplier invoices into the purchase journal and send sales invoices electronically.
- **Intervat**: prepare the VAT return from the booked transactions (see [Intervat](../Intervat/README.md)).
- **Rappels**: print reminders for customers with outstanding invoices.
