# Desktop - AccoWin - Reports

## 1. Introduction

With the reports you print everything you need to check, process and file the bookkeeping: lists, balance sheets, overviews and journal reports. You open the reports from the **Rapporten** (Reports) menu.

The reports are spread over two pages:

- [Lists](Lists/README.md): address lists, journal lists, histories, turnover lists, due-date balance and dossier lists.
- [Balance sheets and overviews](Balance/README.md): trial balance, centralisation, balance sheet, income statement and operating statement.

## 2. How a report is printed

Every report first opens the **Parameters** screen. There you choose:

- the **period** (from ... to) or the **financial years** the report covers;
- the **language** of the report;
- the options of the report, for example the sorting or the number of days between the columns of a due-date balance.

Then click **Afdrukken** (Print). The report appears in a **preview**, where you browse the report, print it or save it as **PDF**.

## 3. Printing several reports at once

In the **Afdrukken** (Print) window (which opens when you select a set of reports) you combine several reports in one print run:

- With **BtnExpand** and **BtnCollapse** you expand and collapse the list of reports and check what you want to print.
- **Start pagina nummer** (Start page number) and **Reset pagina nummer bij elk rapport** (Reset page number per report): the page numbering of the whole bundle or per report.
- **Aangepaste paginanummering** (Custom page numbering), **Huidige datum afdrukken** (Print current date), **Toon paginanummers** (Show page numbers) and **Duplex pagina's toevoegen** (Add duplex pages): the layout of the bundle.
- With **Design** you adjust the layout of the selected report.

## 4. Managing reports (Rapport Design)

Via **Start > Rapport Design** you manage the report definitions in the **Rapporten** (Reports) screen. Per report you enter the **report name** in four languages, the **report type** and the **number of copies**, plus print options such as:

- **Margins (mm)**, **print orientation** and **print logo**;
- **Do not print company details**, **print phone numbers** and **print extra text**;
- **Print amounts**, **print payment condition**, **sales terms** and **print payment QR code** with the **bank account to mention**;
- **Export to PDF: print optimized**;
- **Standard report** or your own layout via **Upload bestand** / **Bewaar bestand** (Upload / Save file).

In the **Rapporten** menu **Accowin** distinguishes the **nieuwe rapporten** (new reports, with the Rapport Design layouts) from the **oude rapporten** (old reports, the classic reports with their fixed parameter screens).

## 5. VAT reports

The VAT-related reports are also under **Rapporten > BT&W**: history of VAT returns, detail of VAT boxes, comparison VAT-turnover and overview of VAT returns. They check the figures behind the return; the filing itself goes via [Intervat](../Intervat/README.md).

## 6. Your own reports and queries

- **Rapporten > Queries**: run saved queries as a report.
- With the report layouts from Rapport Design you build your own reports on top of the standard reports.
