# Desktop - AccoWin - Reports - Balance sheets and overviews

## 1. Introduction

The balance sheets and overviews show the state of the bookkeeping on a date or over a period. All reports first open the **Parameters** screen (see [Reports](../README.md)).

## 2. Trial balance

Via **Rapporten > Proef- en saldibalans** (Trial balance) you print the balances of the **customers**, the **suppliers** or the **general ledger accounts**, with the total movements (debit and credit) of the chosen period. The trial balance is the basic check before the VAT return and the year-end closing.

In the parameters you choose the period with **Selecteer alle periodes** (Select all periods) or **Selecteer boekjaar** (Select financial year), and with the option to add **4 previous financial years** you see the evolution of the balances over several years.

## 3. Centralisation of journals

Via **Rapporten > Centralisatie dagboeken** (Centralisation of journals) you print the totals per general ledger account for every journal: per account you see how much was booked through each journal. Use this report to check the journals against each other.

## 4. Balance sheet

Via **Rapporten > Balans** you print the annual-account-style overviews:

- **Balans en resultatenrekening** (Balance sheet and income statement): the full balance sheet with income statement following the balance model of the dossier.
- **Volledig model** (Full model): all items of the model, also the empty ones.
- **Toelichting balans en resultatenrekening** (Explanation of balance sheet and income statement): the explanation with the detail per balance item.
- **Resultatenrekening** (Income statement): only the result section.
- **Analyse Horizontaal / Verticaal** (Horizontal / vertical analysis): the comparison of the figures against the previous year (horizontal) and the percentage composition of the total (vertical).
- **Exploitatierekening** (Operating statement): the operating result in the classic layout.
- **Exploitatierekening meerdere jaren** (Operating statement multiple years): the evolution over several years.
- **Overzicht Winst/verlies** (Profit/loss overview): the profit and loss overview.

The layout follows the **balance model** linked to the financial year (see [Year end](../../YearEnd/README.md), manage financial years).

## 5. Turnover lists and due-date balance

The **turnover lists** (turnover per customer or supplier) and the **due-date balance** (open items in ageing columns) are described under [Lists](../Lists/README.md).

## 6. Histories

Via **Rapporten > Historieken** you print all transactions per customer, supplier or general ledger account; see [Lists](../Lists/README.md).

## 7. When to use which report

| Report | Typical use |
|---------|-------------|
| Trial balance | monthly check, preparation of the return and the year-end closing |
| Centralisation of journals | check of the mutual relation between the journals |
| Due-date balance | follow-up of the debtors and creditors |
| Balance sheet and income statement | reporting to the customer and the bookkeeper |
| Operating statement (multiple years) | trend analysis of the results |
| Profit/loss overview | quick overview of the result |

## 8. Tips

- Always print the trial balance before the **year-end closing** and keep it with the dossier (see [Year end](../../YearEnd/README.md)).
- With **Analyse Horizontaal / Verticaal** you quickly make a readable overview for the customer.
- The figures in the balance sheets follow the **balance model** of the financial year; choose the right model in **Beheer Boekjaren en Periodes**.
