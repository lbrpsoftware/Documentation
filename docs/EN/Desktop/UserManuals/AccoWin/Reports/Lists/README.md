# Desktop - AccoWin - Reports - Lists

## 1. Introduction

The lists show the master data and the transactions of the dossier. All reports first open the **Parameters** screen (see [Reports](../README.md)).

## 2. Account lists

Via **Rapporten > Rekeningenlijst** (Account lists) you print:

- **Adreslijst Klanten** (Customer address list): address details, phone and email of the customers.
- **Adreslijst Leveranciers** (Supplier address list): the same for the suppliers.
- **Lijst Grootboekrekeningen** (General ledger account list): the chart of accounts with descriptions.

## 3. Journal lists

Via **Rapporten > Dagboeken** (Journals) you print the entered transactions per journal: **Verkopen** (Sales), **Aankopen** (Purchases), **Financieel** (Financial), **Diversen** (Miscellaneous) and **Dagontvangsten** (Daily receipts). In the parameters you choose:

- **Dagboek** (Journal): which journals go into the report. With **Select alles** and **Unselect alles** you check or clear all journals at once.
- **Documenten** (Documents): a range of document numbers, for example `0001-0005, 0007, 0010-0020`.
- **Facturen** (Invoices) or **Alles** (All): only the invoices or all documents.
- **Print remarks**: add the remark of every booking.
- **Kasboek layout** (Cash book layout): a compact cash book layout (for the financial journal).

## 4. Cash book and cash planning

- **Kasboek - Openstaande posten** (Cash book - Open items): the open items of the cash book, with the age of the items.
- **Kasplanning** (Cash planning): a forecast of the payments in and out, based on the due dates of the open invoices.

## 5. Histories

Via **Rapporten > Historieken** (Histories) you print all transactions per **customer**, **supplier** or **general ledger account**. In the parameters you choose among others:

- **Verrichtingen** (Transactions): which transaction types go into the report.
- **Afdrukstand** (Print orientation): portrait or landscape.
- **Sorteren** (Sorting): the sort order.
- **Einde maand** (End of month): add totals per end of month.
- **Nulbedrag** (Zero amount): hide or show accounts without balances.
- **Opmerkingen** (Remarks): print the remarks of the bookings.

## 6. Turnover lists

Via **Rapporten > Omzetlijsten** (Turnover lists) you print the turnover per **customer** or per **supplier** for the chosen period, with the totals.

## 7. Due-date balance

Via **Rapporten > Vervaldagenbalans > Klanten** (or **Leveranciers**) you print the open items in columns by due date:

- **Aantal dagen tussen kolommen** (Number of days between columns): for example 30 for monthly ageing columns.
- **Sorteren** (Sorting): by reference, name or amount.

The balance shows per column how much is open and how old the items are; the totals are at the bottom.

## 8. Dossier lists

Via **Rapporten > Dossiers** you print:

- **Dossierlijst drukken** (Print dossier list): the list of the dossiers of the office.
- **Dossieretiketten drukken** (Print dossier labels): labels for mailing.
- **Historiek info** (History info): statistical information about the dossiers.

## 9. Tips

- Print the journal lists monthly as proof for the filing; together with the **cash planning** they form the monthly check.
- Use the **due-date balance** before every VAT return and before the year-end closing (see [Year end](../../YearEnd/README.md)).
- Most lists can also be saved as **PDF** from the preview.
