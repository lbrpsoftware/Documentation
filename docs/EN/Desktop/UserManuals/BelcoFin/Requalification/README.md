# Desktop - BelcoFin - Rental requalification

## 1. Introduction

When a director rents real estate to his own company, the part of the rent that relates to the **privately used parts** of the property is requalified into **professional income** of the director. The company pays rent for space the director uses (partly) privately — that part of the rent is not taxable property income, but professional income from the director's activity.

**Belcofin** calculates that requalification on the wage record and includes the result in the 274 return and on the fiche 281.20. There is also a **simulation** with which you can calculate the effect before you record it.

## 2. Requalification on the wage record

Open the wage record of the director and go to the tab **Herkwalificatie**. On the list **Huur herkwalificatie** (Rental requalification) you record per period:

- **Professional** (Beroep): the percentage of the property used professionally;
- **Ownership** (Eigendom): the director's share in the property;
- **From** and **to**: the period for which the rent holds;
- **Rent per year** (Huur / jaar): the yearly rent; **indexed** (geïnd) indicates whether the rent is indexed.

From these data **Belcofin** calculates the part of the rent that is requalified as professional income. With the button above the list you print the **detail of the requalification calculation**.

Changes to periods or percentages recalculate the benefit; after every change check the month screen (see [Manual](../Manual/README.md) §5.1.4).

## 3. Rental requalification simulation

With **Simulatie huurherkwalificatie** in the menu you run the effect without changing the record:

1. Choose the **income year** and select the **declarant**.
2. Select the **director**; you set the **name** and the **language** that must appear on the printout.
3. Click **Berekening afdrukken** (Print calculation): **Belcofin** prints the complete requalification calculation of that year.

Use the simulation to estimate the fiscal impact (for example with a changing professional share) before you adapt the data on the wage record.

## 4. Processing

- The requalified amount is added to the income of the right months and counts in the **taxable income** of the 274 return.
- On the **fiche 281.20** the requalified part appears following the rubrics of the tax office, so the director finds it back in his personal tax return.
- The rent the company actually pays remains its own bookkeeping: **Belcofin** processes only the fiscal consequence for the director.

## 5. Tips

- Update the **professional share** in time: a property that goes from private to mixed use halfway the year needs two periods with each their percentage.
- Compare the calculation with the **simulation** before submitting the fiches: a wrong percentage requalifies too much or too little rent.
- Keep the rental contracts with the records; the tax office asks the justification of the percentages used.
